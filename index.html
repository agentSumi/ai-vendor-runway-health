Method

Runway
Estimated cash on hand (latest disclosed) ÷ estimated monthly burn (revenue - net loss, divided across 12 months). Bands: <18 months = high concern · 18-36 months = monitor · >36 months = healthy.
Repricing risk
Shortfall % between the most-recent reported raise's rumoured/target valuation and the actual raise valuation. Bands: ≥50% = high · 25-49% = medium · <25% = low.
Ecosystem dependency
Count of named single-source dependencies: chipmaker (TSMC/NVIDIA), hyperscaler (Azure/GCP/AWS), primary customer (>20% of revenue). Higher = more concentrated risk.
Procurement verdict
Composite of runway + repricing + dependency. Hedge = short contract, dual-vendor. Standard = 12-18 month terms OK. Lock-in OK = healthy runway, low dependency. Not investment advice.
